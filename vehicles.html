LubeLogger

LubeLogger is a simple tracking tool designed to log maintenance intervals, fluid changes, and service history across vehicles. The goal was to create a lightweight system that keeps everything organized in one place without relying on third-party apps or subscriptions.

The project focuses on practical use — tracking oil changes, mileage intervals, and general upkeep — while also serving as a foundation for expanding into more advanced vehicle data logging in the future.
